About the pub

A traditional local with plenty going on.

The Alma is a traditional old-school pub in Sidcup with soul at its heart. It has a beer garden, cocktail bar, function room, real ale, free Wi-Fi and regular entertainment.

We accept major debit and credit cards as well as cash. Cash is king.

The Alma is proud to be an LGBTQ+ friendly venue.

Bookings and events

Book a table, area or event.

The Alma takes bookings in all areas of the pub, from a table for two to an area for larger groups.

For Garden Room bookings, wakes, birthdays, baby showers, wedding parties, family gatherings or private events, please send as much detail as possible.

Email or WhatsApp enquiries are replied to during office hours, until 5pm Monday to Friday.
